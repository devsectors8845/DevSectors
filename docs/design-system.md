# DevSectors design system

## Philosophy: Structured Momentum

DevSectors is presented as an engineering partner, not a decorative creative studio. The visual system uses precise grids, numbered coordinates, clear delivery stages, and strong editorial typography to communicate order and forward motion.

The palette deliberately avoids the pink-purple gradients used by the reference site. Deep ink and mineral blue communicate trust; signal lime marks decisions and actions; warm paper backgrounds keep long-form pages clear and approachable.

Layouts alternate between generous negative space and dense, systematic panels. Rounded corners are restrained, shadows are quiet, and animation supports orientation rather than spectacle. The recurring “sector grid” motif connects the name to a distinct visual idea without relying on stock illustrations.

Typography is direct and compact. Large headlines carry the main idea, while uppercase coordinate labels and monospaced microcopy provide a technical rhythm. Body copy stays plain, specific, and easy to scan.

Accessibility is part of the visual language: strong contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus rings, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and readable line lengths are non-negotiable.

## Interface principles

1. One dominant idea per section.
2. Mobile-first layouts with progressive enhancement at `md` and `lg`.
3. Every control remains usable by keyboard and at 200% zoom.
4. Motion explains state changes and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. Case-study and company claims avoid invented metrics or unverifiable client logos.
